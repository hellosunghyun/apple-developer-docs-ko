CMWaterSubmersionMeasurement.DepthState.unknown
===============================================

device의 depth state를 알 수 없습니다.

iOS 4.0+iPadOS 4.0+Mac Catalyst 4.0+visionOS 1.0+watchOS 2.0+

    case unknown

### [Depth state](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coremotion/cmwatersubmersionmeasurement/depthstate/unknown#Depth-states)

[`case notSubmerged`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coremotion/cmwatersubmersionmeasurement/depthstate/notsubmerged)

device가 물에 잠기지 않은 상태입니다.

[`case submergedShallow`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coremotion/cmwatersubmersionmeasurement/depthstate/submergedshallow)

device가 물에 잠겨 있지만 수심 1미터 미만입니다.

[`case submergedDeep`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coremotion/cmwatersubmersionmeasurement/depthstate/submergeddeep)

device가 수심 1미터 이상 물에 잠긴 상태입니다.

[`case approachingMaxDepth`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coremotion/cmwatersubmersionmeasurement/depthstate/approachingmaxdepth)

device가 최대 안전 잠수 수심에 가까워지고 있습니다.

[`case pastMaxDepth`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coremotion/cmwatersubmersionmeasurement/depthstate/pastmaxdepth)

device가 최대 안전 잠수 수심을 초과했습니다.

[`case sensorDepthError`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coremotion/cmwatersubmersionmeasurement/depthstate/sensordeptherror)

depth sensor에서 error가 발생했습니다.

현재 페이지는 CMWaterSubmersionMeasurement.DepthState.unknown입니다
